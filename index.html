Print / Save Image

PNG = sharpest text. JPEG = smaller file, great for email.

Most email layouts are 600 px wide. 1200 px looks crisp on phones and high-DPI screens.

"Copy" lets you paste the image straight into Outlook or Gmail (Ctrl+V). Readers can click it to go to the Bonus Trivia.

Downloaded files can't contain links. After inserting a downloaded image in your email, select it and press Ctrl+K to add this link.

This link uses localhost, so it only works on your computer. Publish the site and set SITE_URL in data.js so email readers can open it.
